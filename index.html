The problem

Most backtests look good until realistic costs, different market regimes and unseen data are introduced.

Backtests flatter

Strategies are tuned on the same data they are judged on. Entries fill at prices nobody actually received, and costs are an afterthought.

Costs compound quietly

Fees, spread and slippage on every round trip can exceed the entire gross edge of an active strategy — as our own case study shows.

Regimes change

A parameter set that worked in one market period often fails in the next. Walk-forward and sensitivity testing exist to catch exactly that.

What we test

Every engagement runs your strategy through the same validation battery we use on our own research.

Realistic costs

Fees, bid/ask spread, slippage and stop-slippage applied to every fill — always adverse, never optimistic.

Out-of-sample data

The final segment of history is held back and never used for tuning, so the verdict rests on unseen data.

Walk-forward analysis

Parameters are re-optimised on rolling in-sample windows and judged only on the following unseen folds.

Parameter sensitivity

Each parameter is perturbed around your values. A sharp peak surrounded by poor neighbours is the signature of curve-fitting.

Cost stress

The strategy is re-run at 1.5× and 2× costs. A real edge absorbs friction; a fragile one evaporates.

Provider consistency

Where possible, results are cross-checked against an independent market-data source.

Real case study — our own strategy

We ran our in-house BTC/ETH trend strategy through the full battery on two years of real market data (Nov 2024 – Nov 2026, 4-hour candles, 4,375 candles per symbol).

Gross performance (before costs) +6.9%
→
After realistic fees & slippage −0.16%
Walk-forward, out-of-sample −4.77%

Verdict: no robust, repeatable edge.

The system rejected our own strategy rather than producing a flattering result. That is exactly what the system is supposed to do — and it is the standard your strategy will be held to.

FAQ

Is this investment advice?

No. We provide research and analytical services only: we measure how a defined strategy behaved under realistic historical conditions. We do not recommend trades, manage money, or hold client funds.

Will you tell me my strategy is good if it isn't?

No. The service exists because most backtests flatter. If the evidence says there is no robust edge, the report says so — as it did for our own strategy in the case study above.

What do you need from me?

Only what the intake form asks: the strategy rules, market, timeframe, and your testing objectives. Strategy files (Pine Script, Python) help but a precise written specification is enough to start.

Is my strategy kept confidential?

Yes. Submissions are used solely to perform the analysis you ordered and are never shared, resold, or traded against.

How long does a report take?

Typically 3–5 business days per strategy, depending on data availability for your market and timeframe.

What markets do you cover?

BTC and ETH are covered natively with two independent data providers. Other liquid crypto pairs, equities, FX and futures can be accommodated subject to data availability — ask before ordering if unsure.
