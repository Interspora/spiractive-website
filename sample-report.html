SAMPLE · Strategy: trend-following breakout (BTC/ETH) · Window: 2024-11-23 → 2026-11-22 · 4h candles · Report date: 2026-11-22

1. Engagement & methodology

The strategy was evaluated on two years of real market data (4,375 four-hour candles per symbol; 2 minor data gaps, no duplicates) for BTC-USD and ETH-USD. Every fill was charged realistic costs — 0.10% taker fee per side, 0.05% spread, 0.05% slippage per side, and 0.10% additional slippage on stop executions. Orders decided on a candle's close fill no earlier than the next candle's open; no future data was available to any decision. Starting equity: $10,000 (paper).

5. Parameter sensitivity

Each parameter was perturbed between −25% and +25% around its base value (32 valid runs; 0 failed combinations). 11 of 32 neighbours (34%) were positive; the best reached +3.4%, the worst −2.3%. The base configuration sits in a flat, mostly negative neighbourhood — not a robust peak.

7. Data-provider consistency

The identical engine and window cross-checked on an independent provider agreed within 0.4 percentage points (+8.13% vs +7.74% net over the cross-checkable window, 15 trades each). Results are not an artefact of one data source.

Verdict: no robust, repeatable edge after realistic costs.

The evidence converges from five directions: net return ≈ 0 despite a positive gross edge; walk-forward re-optimisation loses out-of-sample; only a third of parameter neighbours are positive; results degrade immediately under higher costs; and the profitable segments coincide with specific favorable market periods. The strategy's genuine strength is defensive — it avoided a −43% buy-and-hold decline with an 11.5% maximum drawdown.
