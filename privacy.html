Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026 · Operator: Interspora Investments LLC

1. What we collect

Only what you give us through the strategy intake form or by email: your name, email address, and the strategy specifications you submit (rules, market, timeframe, and any strategy files you choose to share). This website itself sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics.

2. How we use it

Solely to perform the analysis you ordered, communicate with you about it, and deliver your report. Payment processing is handled by our payment provider (e.g. Stripe); we never see or store your card details.

3. What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share your personal data or your strategy with third parties.
  • We never trade against or publish client strategies.
  • We never use your strategy to train models or improve services for other clients.

4. Retention and deletion

We keep intake records and delivered reports as long as needed for the engagement and legitimate record-keeping. You may request deletion of your personal data and submitted materials at any time by emailing us; we will confirm deletion within 30 days, except where we must retain records by law.

5. Security

Strategy materials are stored on access-controlled systems and handled only by the analyst performing your engagement.
